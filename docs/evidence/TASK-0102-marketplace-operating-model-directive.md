## Human and agent boundary

The operating model permits future specialist agents for high-volume
observation, explanation, controlled hypotheses, simulation, recommendation,
exception routing, and outcome monitoring.

Leadership accountability, policy ownership, legal interpretation, material
capital allocation, irreversible actions, and high-impact authority remain
human by default.

No agent receives sovereign execution authority.

## Compliance intelligence note

The future capability records the opportunity to continuously:

- monitor marketplace rule and policy changes;
- retain authoritative versions and effective dates;
- evaluate current listing compliance;
- distinguish marketplace policy, applicable law, manufacturer guidance, and
  internal policy;
- suggest title, attribute, description, image-text, image, or video changes;
- support complaints, negotiations, and disputes with cited evidence;
- learn whether approved remediation improved operational and economic
  outcomes.

The capability is scheduled by readiness gates. It does not begin until
listing/catalog identity, read-only listing snapshots, governed policy-source
versioning, provenance, effective-time semantics, and human authority ownership
exist.
