# Knowledge Governance

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Active

---

# Purpose

This document defines how knowledge evolves into software within the Flooow Genesis Project.

Every architectural decision shall originate from research and be validated through objective evidence before influencing the Computational Kernel.

# Knowledge Sources

Knowledge may originate from:

- Scientific research
- Real-world organizational observations
- Experimental evidence
- Formal architectural discussions
- Validated implementation experience

Personal opinion alone is not considered a valid source of architectural knowledge.

---

# Knowledge Artifacts

The project adopts the following official artifacts.

## Vision

Defines long-term direction.

---

## Research

Produces scientific investigation.

---

## RFC

Proposes architectural ideas.

RFCs do not change the Kernel.

---

## Experiment

Validates hypotheses through objective evidence.

---

## ADR

Records accepted architectural decisions.

---

## Kernel

Contains only experimentally validated universal concepts.

---

## Applications

Applications validate the Kernel in real-world organizational scenarios.

Applications never define Kernel concepts.

---

# Kernel Evolution Rule

A Kernel concept shall satisfy all of the following conditions:

- Proposed through research or RFC.
- Experimentally validated.
- Supported by reproducible evidence.
- Accepted through architectural decision.

Only then may it become part of the Kernel.

---

# Fundamental Principle

Software is not the source of truth.

Knowledge is the source of software.
